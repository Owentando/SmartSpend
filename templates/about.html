About SmartSpend AI

An AI-powered budgeting assistant for students

🎯 Our Mission

SmartSpend AI helps students save time and money by comparing products across multiple stores — factoring in price, transport cost, distance, and personal preferences.

✨ What We Do

  • 🤖 AI-powered product matching based on your budget
  • 📍 Search results filtered by distance (5km – 100km)
  • 💰 Price comparison across Shoprite, Checkers, Pick n Pay, and more
  • ❤️ Favourites for quick access to products you love
  • 📊 Budget analytics and planning tools
  • 🔔 Back-in-stock alerts for items you want

🎓 Built For

DUT students who want to make the most of their monthly budget. Whether you're planning groceries, toiletries, or study materials — SmartSpend AI has your back.

Tip: Start by clicking a category on your dashboard, then tell us what you need. We'll find the best match within your budget.
